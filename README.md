One line of horror — a single sentence under 200 characters, meant to frighten a reader in one breath. Entry for the [One Line, One Chill](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-one-line-one-chill-6d4d5488) bounty on make.fun.

## The line

> I found my own obituary in tomorrow's newspaper, and the photo was taken from inside my house.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (94 characters, UTF-8, no trailing newline).

Run: `python3 main.py`
